Graphene Field-Effect Transistor (GFET)

Singh Center for Nanotechnology · CVD Transfer · Photolithography · Spring 2026

Fabricated back-gated graphene field-effect transistors at the Singh Center for Nanotechnology at the University of Pennsylvania. This project demonstrated the complete GFET process flow from CVD graphene transfer through to electrical characterization of the finished devices.

Fabrication

CVD-grown monolayer graphene was wet-transferred onto 300 nm SiO₂/Si substrates using a PMMA support layer and copper etchant. Source/drain contacts were patterned via photolithography and deposited by e-beam evaporation (Cr/Au, 5/50 nm), followed by lift-off in warm acetone. Channel regions were defined by a second lithography step and oxygen plasma etch to isolate individual devices.

Characterization

  • Raman Spectroscopy — confirmed monolayer graphene via G/2D peak intensity ratio (I₂D/IG > 2) and absence of D-peak defects
  • Transfer Curves (Ids–Vgs) — ambipolar transport observed with identifiable charge neutrality (Dirac) point
  • Output Curves (Ids–Vds) — linear regime extraction at low bias confirming ohmic contact formation
  • Optical Microscopy — verified graphene coverage uniformity and contact alignment post-fabrication
