MEMS Cantilever Beams & Comb Drive Arrays

Singh Center for Nanotechnology · Photolithography · DRIE · Spring 2026

Fabricated MEMS cantilever beams and comb drive actuators at the Singh Center for Nanotechnology at the University of Pennsylvania. This project explores micromechanical structures used in sensors and actuators.

Fabrication

Devices were fabricated using photolithography, deep reactive-ion etching (DRIE), and selective wet etching on silicon substrates. Process optimization focused on sidewall profiles and etch uniformity.

Characterization

  • SEM — imaging device geometry and etch profiles
  • AFM — surface roughness and step-height measurements
  • TEM — cross-sectional analysis
  • Profilometry — film thickness and etch depth verification
